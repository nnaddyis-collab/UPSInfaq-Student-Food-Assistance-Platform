Our Mission

Small Acts of Kindness. Big Changes.

UPSInfaq is a student food assistance platform designed to connect generous donors with UPSI students who need food support.

✓ Accessible food assistance
✓ Transparent donation management
✓ Stronger university community
✓ Sustainable food support
Simple & Meaningful

How UPSInfaq Works

A simple platform that brings together people who want to give and people who need support.

💚

1. Donate Food

Donors choose food items, select quantities and contribute to the university food fund.

🎓

2. Claim Assistance

Eligible students browse available food, claim assistance and receive a voucher.

🏪

3. Collect Food

Students visit registered stalls and redeem their vouchers to collect food.
